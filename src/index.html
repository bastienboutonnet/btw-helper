    How it works

    • Invoices (Invoice Ninja's invoice report): each invoice's own VAT is used, and the net is its amount less that VAT. The rate it works out to picks the rubriek — 21% 1a, 9% 1b, any other 1c. Billed without VAT, the client's country decides: the Netherlands 1e, another EU country 3b, outside the EU nowhere — a service to a business there is taxed there and stays off the Dutch return. That assumes services: for goods exported outside the EU pick 3a on the invoice, for installations or distance sales taxed in another EU country 3c — any rubriek picked there overrides the rules. Drafts, cancelled and reversed invoices start unticked. VAT is due on the invoice date, paid or not.
    • The file's header row decides how it is read. Invoice Ninja: classification follows Expense Tax Name 1 and VAT is computed at the row's VAT % — the default above unless the tax name implies another or you type one in the table. Bench: each bill's own VAT, its reverse-charge tick and its supplier region are taken as they are, so the rate above is not used; the table shows the rate each bill works out to.
    • Every row in the file counts, so export just the quarter you are filing — in Bench, pick the quarter beside the spreadsheet download. Untick Count on a row that shouldn't be in this return; it stays in the table, greyed, but out of every total and the flags.
    • Reverse Charge → net + computed VAT into 4a (non-EU) or 4b (EU) by vendor country — or 2a for a Dutch supplier (domestic reverse charge); the same VAT also goes into 5b.
    • VAT (NL) (default rate) and any name with reduced, laag or verlaagd in it (9%) → their VAT into 5b only. So does any row you type a VAT % on.
    • Vendor country (Invoice Ninja): your saved map → detected in the vendor name (brackets or after a dash, e.g. Acme – DE) → else you set it in the table.
    • Non-EUR rows use the Converted Amount when present, otherwise Amount × Exchange Rate. A Bench bill given a rate in Bench arrives already in EUR; one without is flagged until you type a rate in the table.
    • 5a is the VAT on sales plus the reverse-charged VAT on purchases; 5c is 5a less 5b.
    • Nothing is filed automatically — this only computes the numbers you type into Mijn Belastingdienst.
